# Blind Validation Protocol

## A. Calibration
Calibration answers are training evidence, not validation.

## B. Freeze
Before blind answers, save exact scenario, prediction, confidence, acceptable response region, expected mechanism, and scoring rule. Do not edit during the run.

## C. Blind answers
Allow free text, unknown, depends, and mixed reactions.

## D. Reveal and score
Only after the whole chain, classify match/partial/deviation, identify error layer, then update.

## E. Cross-person test
Repeat with new people while keeping Generic Engine logic fixed. Person-specific differences belong in Persona Packages unless repeated evidence shows an engine-level failure.
