# Current Research Hypotheses

1. Conditional response regions may be more useful than fixed trait vectors.
2. State can change behavior without rewriting stable traits.
3. Competence and subjective confidence can diverge.
4. Experience often changes process before personality.
5. Relationship history may leave residue after outward repair.
6. Interest and commitment intensity should be separated.
7. A digital self that never changes becomes a snapshot.
8. A digital self that changes too quickly may lose identity continuity.
9. Multiple future branches may each remain valid continuations.
10. Merging divergent branches may create a new identity.
11. Post-presence experiences may change opinions/goals while preserving provenance.
12. Relational witnesses may be fidelity signals, not absolute authorities.
