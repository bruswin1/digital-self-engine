# Contributing

We primarily want falsification and blind-test error reports.

Good contributions include repeated prediction failures, state/trait confusion, competence/confidence confusion, relationship-context failures, and cross-person overfitting.

Do not change the engine solely to fit one participant. Person-specific rules belong in Persona Packages unless repeated independent evidence indicates an engine-level failure.

Label findings as: `external_evidence`, `project_hypothesis`, `exploratory_idea`, `person_specific_evidence`, or `engineering_choice`.
