# Privacy and Data Contribution Rules

Do not upload raw personal data by default. Avoid real names, addresses, contacts, private chats, medical information, financial identifiers, intimate relationship details, and third-party private data.

Preferred contribution: anonymized blind-test error summaries containing only participant ID, case counts, frozen prediction, actual response category, error layer, and a short anonymized explanation.

Keep provenance separate: `self_report`, `behavior_evidence`, `relational_witness`, `model_inference`, `synthetic`.
