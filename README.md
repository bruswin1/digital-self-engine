# Digital Self Engine — Public Research Prototype v0.1

A public research prototype for testing whether one **Generic Engine** can learn substantially different people without hard-coding any participant's private data.

## Separation principle

**Generic Engine** contains mechanisms: state/trait separation, memory, relationship modulation, competence/confidence, process learning, self-narrative, motivation/commitment, identity continuity, provenance, and long-term growth.

**Persona Package** contains person-specific evidence and parameters. Changing people should mean changing the Persona Package, not rewriting the engine.

## Privacy

This repository contains **no Person A private data**: no original answers, memories, relationship details, identifying information, or private validation transcripts.

## How to participate

1. Copy `templates/empty_persona.json`.
2. Calibrate locally.
3. Freeze predictions for unseen scenarios **before** the participant answers.
4. Collect blind answers.
5. Compare prediction vs ground truth only after the chain ends.
6. Submit anonymized error summaries, not raw private data.

## What we want to learn

- Can one engine produce distinct Persona Packages for distinct people?
- Can it predict unseen behavior after calibration?
- Does it avoid pulling everyone toward an average-person template?
- Can it keep learning without freezing or drifting away from identity continuity?

## Status

Research prototype only. It is not a validated psychological instrument, clinical tool, consciousness-transfer system, or proof of identity.
